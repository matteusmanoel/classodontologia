# CLASS Odontologia — Brand Experience

**Status:** v1.1 — Strategic source of truth  
**Purpose:** Define the brand perception, narrative thesis, editorial principles and non-negotiable experience goals for the CLASS Odontologia website.  
**Audience:** Product owner, creative direction, Cursor agents, designers, developers, reviewers and QA agents.
**Production domain:** `https://classodontologia.com.br`  
**Clinic founded:** `25/03/1998`

---

## 1. Strategic objective

The website must do more than present a premium dental clinic.

Its job is to create a strong, coherent perception:

> **“This clinic has history, masters aesthetics and technology, pays obsessive attention to detail and seems to operate at another level.”**

The website must feel closer to browsing a high-end editorial publication than navigating a conventional clinic website.

Motion, typography, photography, interaction and technology are not goals by themselves. They exist only to increase at least one of the following:

1. Brand perception.
2. Narrative clarity.
3. Proof of competence.
4. Conversion.

If an intervention only “looks cool”, it belongs in a spike or laboratory, not automatically in production.

---

## 2. Core brand thesis

### CLASS is not only the clinic name. CLASS is the standard.

The website should progressively communicate that CLASS represents a particular way of practicing dentistry.

This standard is built from:

- experience;
- criterion;
- precision;
- continuous technological updating;
- aesthetic judgment;
- human execution.

The strategic idea is:

> **The standard evolves. The CLASS way remains.**

This sentence is a strategic principle, not necessarily final public copy.

The objective is not to define CLASS as luxury for luxury’s sake.

The intended interpretation is:

> **CLASS represents high standards because there is experience, rigor, precision, environment, technology and professional judgment capable of sustaining that position.**

---

## 3. What CLASS should not become

The site must avoid becoming another generic “premium dentistry” website.

Avoid positioning based mainly on:

- luxury;
- exclusivity;
- “perfect smile” language;
- generic excellence claims;
- excessive glamour;
- equipment catalogs;
- corporate team grids;
- decorative animation;
- cliché before/after aesthetics as the primary identity;
- copy such as “technology, excellence and humanized care” without proof.

The brand may look expensive and refined without repeatedly saying it is luxurious.

Sophistication should be inferred from execution.

---

## 4. Narrative pillars

The brand is organized around five connected pillars.

They are **not five independent benefits**. They are five forms of evidence for one central thesis: **there is a CLASS standard of dentistry.**

### 4.1 Legacy — experience creates criterion

Legacy must not be treated as nostalgia.

The message is not:

> “We have existed for many years.”

The intended meaning is:

> **Long experience created judgment, maturity and a higher standard for decision-making.**

The past legitimizes the present.

The narrative should therefore move from:

**origin → evolution → current mastery**

The family relationship between Dr. Alessandro, Dra. Renata and Dr. Matheus may support the story, but the site should avoid a “dynasty” or “knowledge handed from generation to generation” narrative as its main frame.

Preferred framing:

> **A standard built through experience and continuously updated.**

The newer generation, new techniques and new technologies do not replace the standard. They expand it.

#### Historical fact — confirmed

CLASS Odontologia was founded on **25 March 1998** (`25/03/1998`).

Public copy must not claim “more than three decades” before that statement is factually true. Prefer durable forms that do not require annual maintenance, such as:

- **“Desde 1998.”**
- **“Um padrão construído desde 1998.”**
- other historically accurate language anchored to the founding date.

The date may be used editorially as `25 / 03 / 1998`, `EST. 1998` or `Desde 1998`, depending on the Scene Contract.

---

### 4.2 Precision — the central code of the brand

Precision is the strongest unifying concept in the system.

It connects:

- clinical precision;
- diagnosis;
- planning;
- execution;
- aesthetics;
- technology;
- typography;
- spacing;
- animation;
- interface behavior.

The website must **demonstrate precision**, not merely claim it.

The visitor should feel that every movement, alignment and transition was deliberate.

Motion should therefore feel:

- measured;
- controlled;
- calibrated;
- intentional;
- restrained.

Avoid movement that communicates playfulness, chaos or gratuitous spectacle.

The digital product itself is part of the proof.

---

### 4.3 Aesthetics — proportion, balance and naturalness

Aesthetics should not be reduced to beauty or cosmetic appeal.

The preferred conceptual territory is:

- proportion;
- form;
- balance;
- structure;
- identity;
- naturalness;
- detail.

The strongest visual references may therefore come from architecture, industrial design, editorial design and fine material studies as much as from conventional dental advertising.

A tooth may be treated almost as a sculptural object.

A face may communicate proportion and identity rather than simply serving as a “before/after” vehicle.

The intended message is:

> **Aesthetics is the consequence of judgment and precision, not ornamentation.**

---

### 4.4 Technology — invisible infrastructure for predictability

Technology should not dominate the narrative through lists of equipment.

The patient does not primarily buy a scanner, software or device.

The patient buys the confidence that the case was:

- seen better;
- planned better;
- controlled better;
- executed with greater predictability.

Technology therefore acts as infrastructure for precision.

Preferred principle:

> **Show technological sophistication; do not advertise technology for its own sake.**

When named technologies, brands or systems are presented, they must reinforce a clear clinical or strategic reason.

---

### 4.5 People — humanity behind the standard

Without the people layer, legacy + precision + aesthetics + technology can become emotionally cold.

The people pillar should create the human turn of the narrative.

The preferred sequence is:

1. establish the standard;
2. demonstrate mastery;
3. reveal who sustains it.

Professionals should not initially appear as a generic HR grid.

Their role is to answer:

> **“Who am I trusting?”**

Photography should favor editorial confidence and proximity over exaggerated corporate smiles.

The narrative should communicate continuity and renewal:

- experience established the standard;
- new techniques and professionals keep the standard current;
- different competencies operate under the same CLASS way of doing dentistry.

---

## 5. Brand meaning of “CLASS”

The name can carry positive associations with:

- class;
- category;
- distinction;
- standard;
- refinement;
- quality threshold.

However, the site should deliberately move beyond status alone.

The desired understanding is:

> **CLASS is a standard of execution.**

### 5.1 Brand platform

The strategic platform is:

> **O padrão Class.**

This is the central editorial territory used to organize the website narrative. It is not required to appear verbatim in every section.

### 5.2 Internal narrative principle

> **O padrão evolui. O jeito Class permanece.**

This remains a strategic/internal principle. It expresses continuity with renewal and should guide editorial decisions even when the exact sentence is not exposed publicly.

### 5.3 Public tagline — V1 decision

> **A arte de sorrir com Class.**

Use **Class** as the brand name rather than the generic noun “classe”. This preserves the status/refinement association while making the phrase proprietary to the brand.

Recommended role:

- emotional signature;
- closing statement;
- selective brand communication.

Do not require the tagline to explain the full positioning. The website earns its meaning through the “Padrão Class” narrative and may use the tagline as an emotional conclusion.

### 5.4 Approved conceptual territory

- “O padrão Class”;
- “O jeito Class”;
- “Selected by Class”;
- “Um padrão. Diferentes especialidades.”;
- “A arte de sorrir com Class.”

These expressions have different roles and must not be mechanically repeated in every viewport.

## 6. Desired visitor perception by the end of the homepage

The visitor should leave with the following impressions:

1. **This clinic has substance and history.**
2. **Its standard is deliberate, not improvised.**
3. **Aesthetics is treated with judgment, not trend-chasing.**
4. **Technology is integrated into the method.**
5. **The professionals appear credible and current.**
6. **The physical clinic and service feel real, refined and attainable.**
7. **There is a distinct CLASS way of practicing dentistry.**

---

## 7. Editorial principle

The website should behave more like a premium editorial publication than a conventional service catalog.

Consequences:

- fewer words;
- stronger headlines;
- large visual moments;
- generous whitespace;
- progressive disclosure;
- restrained navigation;
- visual hierarchy before information density;
- motion used to transform composition, not decorate components;
- sections that feel like acts or spreads, not stacked cards.

Copy should be short enough to work as design material.

A visitor should be able to understand the emotional argument even without reading every supporting sentence.

---

## 8. Emotional progression

The current dark-to-light transition should be treated as narrative, not simply styling.

### Dark territory

Represents:

- abstraction;
- precision;
- object;
- technology;
- mystery;
- control;
- brand identity.

### Light / paper territory

Represents:

- history;
- humanity;
- knowledge;
- people;
- physical space;
- confidence;
- accessibility.

Preferred emotional progression:

> **abstraction → mastery → evidence → humanity → reality**

This progression should guide sequence decisions throughout the homepage.

---

## 9. Three hero moments

The site should not attempt to make every section spectacular.

The preferred experience uses three memorable high-intensity moments separated by quieter editorial passages.

### Hero Moment 01 — Identity

Existing cinematic hero.

Purpose:

- establish difference;
- signal precision;
- establish CLASS as a brand before presenting services.

Current strategy: **preserve and refine only when necessary.**

Do not continuously expand hero complexity while the rest of the experience remains weaker.

### Hero Moment 02 — The standard applied

Specialties.

Purpose:

- demonstrate breadth without becoming a service catalog;
- show that different disciplines operate under one CLASS standard.

Core concept:

> **One standard. Multiple disciplines.**

### Hero Moment 03 — The people behind the standard

Specialists.

Purpose:

- humanize the experience;
- transform authority into trust;
- communicate continuity + renewal.

Potential visual territory: light, shadow, focus and editorial portraits.

## 10. Technologies and selected brands

Brands and systems such as Invisalign, Odontolatina, Ortoplan and others may reinforce the technology/criterion narrative, but each name must be classified correctly before public claims are made.

Do not automatically call every brand a “partner”.

### 10.1 Preferred public territory

**Eyebrow / section label:**

> **SELECTED BY CLASS**

**Working headline:**

> **Escolhido com critério.**

**Working supporting copy:**

> **Tecnologias e marcas presentes em nossa prática clínica, selecionadas de acordo com cada indicação.**

This wording is intentionally conservative: it communicates selection without implying a formal institutional partnership.

### 10.2 Relationship classification requirement

Before each external brand is published, classify the relationship as one of the following or another explicitly verified category:

- technology/system used in clinical practice;
- supplier/manufacturer;
- accreditation/certification;
- formal partner;
- historical/brand relationship;
- other verified relationship.

Specific wording may then be enriched when the relationship is documented.

### 10.3 Current names under review

- Invisalign;
- Odontolatina;
- Ortoplan;
- other aesthetic/clinical technologies to be confirmed later.

**Ortoplan requires contextual validation before being displayed in the same category as a technology supplier or system.**

The point of this act is not logo accumulation. The point is to communicate selection and criterion.

## 11. Conversion principle

The site should earn the conversion request before asking for it aggressively.

Primary conversion should feel like the natural next step after trust is built.

Preferred tone:

- start a conversation;
- schedule an evaluation;
- speak with the clinic;
- other direct but refined CTAs.

Avoid repeated aggressive CTAs in every viewport.

---

## 12. Strategic non-negotiables

Any future feature, scene, copy proposal or visual change must be evaluated against these principles:

1. Does it reinforce the CLASS standard?
2. Does it communicate precision rather than gratuitous spectacle?
3. Does it add evidence, clarity, trust or conversion value?
4. Does it preserve editorial restraint?
5. Does it fit the emotional progression of the page?
6. Can the visitor understand the intended message without an explanation from the designer?
7. Is it technically justified relative to its complexity?

If the answer is no, reconsider or remove it.

## 13. Decisions and remaining gates

### 13.1 Decided for V1

- public tagline: **“A arte de sorrir com Class.”**;
- strategic brand platform: **“O padrão Class.”**;
- founding date: **25/03/1998**;
- production domain/canonical target: **`https://classodontologia.com.br`**;
- homepage specialty set:
  1. Prótese;
  2. Estética;
  3. Implantodontia;
  4. Periodontia;
  5. Sensibilidade;
  6. Ortodontia;
  7. Cirurgias;
  8. ATM — Disfunção Temporomandibular;
- professional information: preserve the currently verified repository content for now; additional credentials may be added later, but agents must not invent them;
- typography direction: **Geist Sans + Instrument Serif**, with **Geist Mono** permitted only as a limited utility/metadata voice; final implementation values remain subject to the Typography Spike;
- selected-brands wording direction: selection/criterion language rather than unverified “partner” claims.

### 13.2 Remaining Decision Gates

Implementation agents must not silently resolve the following:

- final public copy after real layout/composition review;
- final line breaks and text hierarchy per responsive viewport;
- final order/pacing of the eight specialties inside the interactive scene;
- exact relationship classification and approved logo usage for each external brand/technology;
- final photographic and AI-generated asset inventory;
- final asset approval, crops and truth classification (`real`, `representative`, `abstract`, `decorative`);
- exact typography scale, weights, tracking and responsive behavior after the Typography Spike;
- additional professional credentials or specialty labels not already verified in the repository.

### 13.3 Asset planning requirement

A dedicated `ASSET_PLAN.md` must be created before production asset generation. Each asset should define at minimum:

- asset ID;
- scene;
- narrative role;
- subject/content;
- source (`real photography`, `AI generated`, `existing`, `graphic`);
- truth classification (`real`, `representative`, `abstract`, `decorative`);
- desktop/mobile aspect ratios;
- master dimensions;
- output formats;
- lighting/background direction;
- safe areas for typography;
- motion/crop tolerance;
- accessibility/alt intent;
- generation restrictions;
- approval status.

The interface must not present AI-generated representative people, treatments or outcomes as factual patient cases or real professionals.

## 14. Source-of-truth hierarchy

When documents conflict, use this priority:

1. Explicit product-owner decision.
2. `BRAND_EXPERIENCE.md`.
3. `EXPERIENCE_BLUEPRINT.md`.
4. `MOTION_AND_TYPE_SYSTEM.md`.
5. Approved Scene Contract.
6. Technical implementation convenience.

Technical convenience never overrides approved brand strategy without explicit review.
