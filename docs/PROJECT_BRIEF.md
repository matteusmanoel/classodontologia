# Project Brief — Class Odontologia

## Brand

**Class Odontologia** is the rebranding of the former Ortoplan clinic presence in Foz do Iguaçu, Paraná, Brazil.

## Positioning

- Premium, sophisticated, high-authority
- Class A / affluent audience
- Clinical excellence and refinement
- Confidence and modernity — enduring, not trendy
- Fundamentally distinct from the generic visual identity of the previous brand

## Audience

Brazilian Portuguese-speaking residents and visitors of Foz do Iguaçu. Primary audience: class A/B adults seeking specialist dental care.

## Product Objective

This is a **premium institutional landing-page MVP**. It is not a rebuild of the old website.

Its purpose is to prove:
- Visual language and cinematic interaction
- Technical foundation and performance
- Mobile experience and brand positioning
- Future extensibility

Prioritize:
- clarity over sophistication
- performance over quantity of effects
- simplicity over unnecessary abstraction
- testable MVP over theoretically complete website

## MVP Information Architecture

Essential sections (must ship):

1. **Hero** — Tooth cinematic + Wordmark + decade beat + Hero copy lines
2. **Manifesto** — Positioning on paper; Smile cinematic in a dark well
3. **Specialties** — Clinical services overview
4. **Specialists** — Portrait-forward Specialist cards
5. **Location** — Address + Map art (not an embed)
6. **Appointment CTA** — Primary conversion path
7. **Footer** — Compact contact strip (address remains secondary)

Optional sections (ship when real assets/copy are approved):

- Testimonials
- Clinic / Technology / Facility (interior, equipment — distinct from Location)
- Results / Before & After

Post-MVP (explicitly excluded from this implementation):

- Blog / editorial content
- Individual doctor profile pages
- Patient portal / online booking
- CMS integration
- Multi-language
